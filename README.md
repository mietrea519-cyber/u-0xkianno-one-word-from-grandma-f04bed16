Grandma heard her grandchild has been using ChatGPT — and her brain filed it under the one school word she actually knows. So the whole text she sends is just its name, the way she heard it: **ChatGPA**. One swapped letter, and suddenly the AI isn't a chatbot at all, it's a grade she's worried about. That's the entry: a single word that is warm, a little baffled, and fretting about school, exactly like a real grandmother texting her grandchild about AI.

Competition: [One Word From Grandma](https://make.fun/c/u-0xkianno-one-word-from-grandma-f04bed16)

Run `node main.ts` and it writes the one word to `/output/entry.txt`.
